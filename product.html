Privlekai, screen by screen

What a small business owner sees, in the order they see it — and which Google Ads API operations sit behind each screen.

These are design mock-ups, not screenshots of shipped software. Privlekai is in development; the screens below show the interface as designed and are published so that the product can be evaluated before it is generally available. Interface language is English or Russian; the example throughout is a real category of Kazakhstani small business — a window repair company in Almaty. Keywords appear in Russian because that is what its customers type, with an English gloss underneath.

1. Is advertising worth starting at all?

Demand research · before any money is committed

The owner describes the business in their own words. Privlekai finds the words customers actually search for, retrieves volume and cost estimates for each, and gives a straight answer — including “no” when the answer is no.

2. The campaign, with the reasoning attached

Creation · proposal, not yet created

Every setting is shown with a sentence explaining why it was chosen and what it costs the owner. Advertising tools normally show settings; Privlekai's whole point is that it shows the reasoning, because its user does not know what the settings mean.

Why no target CPA yet: a target given before the account has conversion history makes Google throttle your ads to almost nothing. We set the target on day 15, from your own numbers rather than a guess.
Why the Display Network is off: your customer has a broken window right now. Showing them a banner while they read the news does not sell a repair, but it does spend the budget.
Why 08:00–20:00: a call at 23:00 that nobody answers is a paid click you have lost. Your team answers between 08:00 and 20:00, so that is when we advertise.

What this means
Budget, not creative. Your ads convert at 2.8% and your cost per enquiry has been flat for three weeks — the campaign is not broken, it is capped. Reporting data is retrieved on request and is never more than 24 hours old. Where Google itself has not yet finalised a figure, the screen says so rather than showing a number that will change.

What is deliberately not here

  • Privlekai never takes a percentage of advertising spend, and never becomes the payer of a customer's ad account.
  • Privlekai does not consolidate customers into a single manager account it controls. Each business connects its own account and keeps it.
  • Privlekai does not act on money without a human confirmation, and does not hide what it changed.
